## Product-claim discipline

If R5 requires central vulnerability triage or MR presentation, clarify the **specific** capability, entitlement, supported configuration and required evidence. S5/S6 establish selected documentary distinctions; they are not a trial result or a commercial offer. No claim is made about data residency, regulatory compliance, private-network fit, licensing price, guaranteed savings, roadmap delivery or competitive superiority.

Lab 01's measured results remain in [its verification record](../../VERIFICATION.md). The scenario, personas, R1–R9 relationships, G1–G8 journey, priorities and gap assessment are explicitly hypothetical or proposed analysis. No external claim is used to fill an unknown buyer field.
