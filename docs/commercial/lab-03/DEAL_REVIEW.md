## One-minute review

“The platform lead is hypothetically enthusiastic, but I have no validated economic authority, champion, budget, accepted criteria or purchasing path. Lab 01 demonstrates technical controls for the portfolio; it is not this buyer's acceptance. My next proposal would be a buyer-approved roles and decision-path review with the platform lead and relevant sponsor, then an appropriately scoped evaluation only if the problem warrants one. I would make the assurance and procurement dependencies visible early, retain the option of keeping GitHub, and avoid describing this as a committed purchase.”

This is proposed language for an interview. No review meeting or next step has actually been arranged.

## Seller-team coordination to propose

| Seller-side role, if engaged | Purpose and boundary |
| --- | --- |
| Account executive | Coordinate the buyer-owned case, stakeholder coverage and agreed next actions; do not infer authority or promise an approval date |
| Solutions architect | Help scope authorised technical validation and map evidence to buyer criteria; no blanket product or security assurance |
| Customer-success counterpart | Plan a clear success/adoption handoff when appropriate; no claim of implemented value or an assigned team |
| Partner specialist / implementation adviser | Participate only if an actual partner route or service need is established; clarify contracting and ownership |

GitLab's [public enterprise sales handbook](https://handbook.gitlab.com/handbook/sales/playbook/) describes cross-functional deal-team collaboration. The suggested coordination here is portfolio analysis; no GitLab representative, partner or customer-success team has participated in this project.

## Three changes that should alter the assessment

- **The potential champion moves role:** revalidate relationships, ownership and the decision path. Do not assume interest or authority transfers to a successor.
- **Security cannot approve the evidence:** identify the actual missing requirement and owner. Narrow the evaluation or stop if a mandatory condition cannot be met; do not bypass the reviewer or promise an untested feature.
- **Funding remains unavailable:** separate technical learning from purchasing intent. Revisit scope or timing only with the buyer's agreement, or recommend deferring/retaining the current approach.

The recommendation remains conditional on evidence. Existing [Lab 02 objections](../lab-02/OBJECTIONS.md) and [cost/pilot tools](../lab-02/README.md) remain the resources for those discussions, not a repeated deck in this lab.
