## How each example would change the conversation

- **A01:** the possible platform-lead value hypothesis is consistent evidence and manageable ownership. Reuse Lab 02's evaluation criteria; do not assert that effort is already reduced.
- **B01:** an AppSec hypothesis needs an exact reporting/triage requirement and entitlement check. Generic CLI reports from Lab 01 do not prove the paid workflow was evaluated.
- **C01:** acknowledge what works and stop/defer when no gap exists. There is no draft outreach for C01 in the proposed sequence.
- **D01:** resolve or rule out the mandatory mismatch through appropriate technical review before any evaluation proposal. No draft outreach is assigned to D01.
