# Target-account selection and transparent prioritisation

**Proposed portfolio method, not GitLab's official ICP, territory rules or lead score.** The inputs below are hypotheses until supported by appropriate evidence. No real account research or targeting list has been assembled.

## Selection criteria

| ID | Criterion | Weight | Evidence to seek in an authorised future assessment | What must not be inferred |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| C1 | A relevant delivery/evidence problem | 30 | Buyer-confirmed recurring burden or a clearly labelled public signal to investigate | A technology choice alone does not establish pain or intent |
| C2 | Delivery coordination complexity | 20 | Multiple workflows/teams and the actual ownership/integration burden | Size or headcount does not automatically mean value |
| C3 | Control/evidence requirements | 20 | Actual control, review, retention and findings-workflow requirements | Industry labels do not prove a compliance need or product entitlement |
| C4 | Readiness to consider change | 15 | Buyer-owned initiative, resources and acceptable disruption | A public hiring post or tool mention is not an agreed project |
| C5 | A viable evaluation/commercial path | 15 | Accessible decision process, sponsorship and review route | A friendly technical contact is not authority, budget or a champion |

Weights total **100**. Ratings use an explicitly illustrative 0–4 scale: 0 = mismatch, 1 = weak fit, 2 = plausible partial fit, 3 = strong fit if evidenced, 4 = strongest fit if evidenced. Evidence quality is recorded separately; an invented rating is never treated as a buyer confirmation. No sensitive personal characteristics are used.

```text
Weighted score out of 100 = sum(weight × rating / 4)
```

Every criterion must have a numeric rating for a complete score. **Unknown stays blank and returns Incomplete**, rather than becoming zero. Evidence sources, dates and confidence should accompany ratings. A score is a transparent discussion aid, not a win probability, purchase recommendation or proof of urgency.

## Gates before any proposed activity

1. **Mandatory fit = No:** exclude regardless of weighted score. Do not promise an unavailable capability to keep an account in the model.
2. **Problem confirmed = No:** defer; do not manufacture pain.
3. **Any gate unknown, or any rating incomplete:** research/clarification only. No outreach-ready classification is assigned.
4. **All inputs complete and gates Yes:** human review only, including actual territory/account ownership, duplicate-account checks, channel suitability and any required organisational approval. The calculator never recommends or sends a message.

Gate values refer to a possible future authorised process. In this portfolio, examples remain fictional and **all contact is out of scope**, even when a counterfactual input is Yes. Public material may suggest a research question; it does not establish outreach permission, personal consent, funding or a buying event.

Use [prioritisation-template.csv](prioritisation-template.csv) for blank candidates and [ACCOUNT_EXAMPLES.md](ACCOUNT_EXAMPLES.md) for invented illustrations. Apply gates before reviewing a ranking; compare only complete scores within a comparable scope. Break ties through evidence quality/freshness and relevant ownership constraints, not additional unsupported precision.
