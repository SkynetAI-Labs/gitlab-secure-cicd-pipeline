# Hypothetical 30/60/90-day execution plan

**Not executed. No assigned GitLab territory, employer quota, customer contact, campaign or achieved result is represented.** The plan is an interview exercise showing learning, prioritisation and review discipline. Real scope and measures would need agreement with a future manager; nothing here schedules outreach.

| Period | Proposed focus | Hypothetical work and deliverables | Evidence gate / success measure |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–30 | Learn and establish a defensible account view | Study the public product/sales material, understand actual territory/account ownership if in an authorised role, calibrate the selection rubric, and draft evidence-labelled account hypotheses | Reviewed capability/entitlement boundaries; complete source notes; unknowns visible; no unsupported intent or authority claims |
| Days 31–60 | Test a narrow, approved approach | In a future authorised setting only, propose a small reviewed cohort and buyer-relevant message variants; establish account-level tracking and stop rules. In this portfolio, perform only draft and model review. | Human approval before any live action; relevant positioning, accurate identity, deduplicated denominators and reviewable next steps; no claimed responses or meetings |
| Days 61–90 | Review, adapt or stop | Compare actual evidence with assumptions only if a legitimate pilot ever occurred; otherwise keep outputs as untested hypotheses. Examine failure reasons, capacity and buying-process gaps before proposing scale. | Calibrate assumptions rather than declare success; stop/defer poor-fit activity; document learning and accountable handoff |

The plan is chronological learning and review, not a promise of a 90-day enterprise sale. ## Illustrative workload envelope

The separate [model](FUNNEL_MODEL.md) assumes 100 screened account placeholders over 90 days, 60 hypothetical research-fit accounts, and 30 hypothetically eligible accounts. These are mathematical inputs, not work completed or people selected. A maximum five-step sequence implies at most 150 modelled actions before stop rules reduce the total.

The capacity example assumes 80 available hours for this bounded pilot allocation across the 90-day window—not an account executive's entire working time—with 72.5 hours assigned to research, drafting, hypothetical maximum touches, review/admin and a conversation/preparation reserve. These are invented effort assumptions, not a productivity benchmark or a measured schedule. There are no send dates or enrolled recipients.

## Execution boundaries

Do not equate activity volume with buyer value. Meaningful progress requires validated problems, appropriate ownership and an agreed next step—not a positive model score alone. The [success measures](SUCCESS_MEASURES.md) distinguish quality, conditional conversion and actual commercial outcomes. No revenue, customer, won-deal, achieved-meeting or paid-feature result is claimed.
