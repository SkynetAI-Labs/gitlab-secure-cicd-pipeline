1. **Researched facts:** the limited vendor statements above, linked at their use in [positioning](POSITIONING.md).
2. **Measured portfolio proof:** the recorded configured pipeline in [Lab 01](../../VERIFICATION.md), with its external CLI and production-assurance limits.
3. **Fictional account assumptions:** all A01–D01 situations, ratings and gate values in [ACCOUNT_EXAMPLES.md](ACCOUNT_EXAMPLES.md). No real company or person was researched for these examples.
4. **Proposed execution / mathematical expectations:** the unsent drafts, 30/60/90 plan, prioritisation weights, funnel rates and capacity values. None is a campaign result or official GitLab operating commitment.

No numeric vendor prices, compliance certification, roadmap promise, guaranteed security detection, data-residency claim, competitive superiority, customer quotation or achieved commercial result is asserted.
