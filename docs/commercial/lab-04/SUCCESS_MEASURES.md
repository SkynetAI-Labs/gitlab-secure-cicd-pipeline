# Success measures and review discipline

**Proposed measures, not observed campaign results.** There was no campaign, outreach or buyer pilot. Actual commercial metrics are not collected. The portfolio's observable results are the authored materials, calculation checks, rendering and CI verification—not customer or sales outcomes.

| Measure | Definition / denominator | What a legitimate future review would establish |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Evidence quality | Complete source/status notes divided by reviewed account hypotheses | Whether a rating is supported, current and labelled appropriately; do not count fictional examples as researched accounts |
| Selection discipline | Reviewed candidates with complete ratings and explicit gate disposition | Whether hard mismatches are excluded and unknowns remain visible; score alone does not establish eligibility |
| Message quality | Reviewed drafts with relevant hypothesis, accurate identity, bounded claim and proportionate next step | Quality before volume; no claim of personalisation based on invented events |
| Reply / positive response | Unique replying accounts / eligible accounts; positive accounts / replying accounts | Account-level deduplication, consistent window and response definitions; no reliance on email opens |
| Conversation progression | Accepted accounts / positive accounts; held accounts / accepted accounts | No conflation of offers, accepted times and held conversations; fractions in the model are not achieved meetings |
| Qualification | Buyer-validated evaluation accounts / held-conversation accounts | A real problem, appropriate owner and mutually agreed next step, using existing [Lab 03 gaps](../lab-03/QUALIFICATION_GAPS.md) |
| Capacity and learning | Time by task, constraints and reasons for stop/defer | Whether the approach is sustainable and assumptions need revision |

Review at the 30/60/90 checkpoints. Separate seller hypotheses, model expectations and buyer-confirmed evidence. Low sample sizes cannot justify a broad conversion or performance claim. Where evidence is absent, record **Not measured**, not a fabricated result or zero-conversion campaign.

No automatic revenue attribution, quota attainment, pipeline value, win rate, customer count or delivered ROI is included. Before any real recommendation, use [Lab 02's evaluation/economics](../lab-02/README.md) and [Lab 03's buying process](../lab-03/BUYING_PROCESS.md). A decision to retain current tools or stop poor-fit work is a valid learning outcome.
